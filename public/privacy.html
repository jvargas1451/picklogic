PickLogic Privacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026

PickLogic is a free entertainment tool for Powerball and Mega Millions players. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

  • Account information: your email address and password. Your password is handled by our authentication provider and is never visible to us in readable form.
  • What you enter: ticket numbers, draw dates, optional amounts wagered and won, and notes.
  • Username: optional. If you set one, it is shown publicly on the leaderboard with your point total.
  • Activity: points you earn and daily check-ins.
  • Technical data: our hosting provider automatically records basic request information, such as IP address and browser type, as part of normal operation.

How we use it

To run your account, save and check your tickets against official results, show your stats, and display the leaderboard. We do not sell your information, share it with advertisers, or show ads.

Who processes it

Your data is stored with Supabase (database and login) and the site is hosted on Vercel. Both act on our behalf.

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address and browser type when a page loads.

Cookies and storage

We store a login session in your browser so you stay signed in. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account exists. To delete your account and everything associated with it (tickets, points, and username), email picklogichelp@gmail.com from the address on your account. We will complete the deletion within 30 days.

Age

PickLogic is intended for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above.
